The Artist

Hello, I’m Sara.

A resin artist from the beautiful island of Madeira, Portugal.

Art and decoration have always been part of my life — a family passion passed down through generations.

Inspired by my love of Nature, Madeira and its charming villages, I create tiny pieces of jewellery that capture a little moment in time.
